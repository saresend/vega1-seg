    01what is this

    Vega1-seg finds an object in a video and makes a mask of it. You write what to find, for example "the dog". The model marks the pixels of the dog on each frame.

    The model operates fully on your device, in this browser tab. It does not send the video or your words to a server. It operates on a laptop or on a phone.

    Use the mask to edit the video: to put the object in front of a new background, to blur it, or to follow it with a box.

    02how it works

    frame ──> 6 tiles ──> image encoder ──> features ─┐
                                                  ├─> mask decoder ──> mask
"the dog" ──────────> text encoder ──> query ─────┤         ▲
                                                  └─> Vega1: found? ┘
    • tiles The model divides each frame into 6 square tiles. Small objects stay large enough to see.
    • encoder The MobileCLIP-S0 image encoder (11 million parameters) examines each tile one time. The model keeps the result, so a new question does not repeat this step.
    • found? Vega1, our yes/no model, examines the full frame and its tiles and decides if the object is in the frame. The mask shows only when Vega1 agrees. This removes most masks of objects that are not there.
    • objects The model divides each mask into separate objects and follows each object from frame to frame. Click an object to remove it from the mask, or to put it back.
    • decoder A small decoder (0.7 million parameters) compares the tile with your words. It makes a mask of 64 × 64 points for each tile and gives the probability that the object is in the tile. The model joins the tiles into one mask for the frame.
    • training We trained the decoder on 100,000 questions about objects in 12,500 COCO images, with masks that people drew. The questions use many words for each object ("dog", "puppy") and words for groups ("animal", "vehicle"), so the decoder follows the meaning of your words.
    –for each new question, after the model examines the video
    –average overlap with masks that people drew (test images)
    –correct "not found" answers when the object is not in the image
    0 bytessent to a server: all work occurs on your device

    03limits

    • The mask is not exact at the edges. It is good for a box, a blur or a rough cut-out. It is not sufficient for exact rotoscoping.
    • The model knows best the 80 types of objects in COCO (people, animals, vehicles, furniture, food and more). For types of objects that it did not see in training, the masks are less accurate (23% overlap, against 54%).
    • The preview examines 16 frames. Between them, the mask moves smoothly but it does not follow fast motion. The export examines every frame.
    • Sometimes the model finds an object that is not there, for example a dog in a video of a cat. Click the object to remove it from the mask.
    • The model finds all objects of the type that you write. It cannot yet select one object from a group ("the dog on the left").
